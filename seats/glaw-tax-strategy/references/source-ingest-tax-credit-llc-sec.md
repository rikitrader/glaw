## Ingest Conclusions

1. GLAW must separate three workstreams that converge in a founder/unicorn matter:
   tax qualification, capital-raising disclosure, and wealth/asset sheltering.
2. QSBS advice is not file-ready unless the corporation maintains an issuer-side
   substantiation packet and gives shareholders a consistent information statement.
3. A trust-owned investment LLC is a useful default for diversified investments, but
   founder/QSBS stock, restricted stock, S-corp stock, retirement assets, insurance, and
   issuer-restricted securities require direct-ownership analysis before any transfer.
4. Investor-facing capital raises must include securities exemption analysis, risk factors,
   investor suitability/accreditation/QP/QC status, tax disclaimers, transfer restrictions,
   ongoing reporting promises, and a clean source-of-funds/KYC/OFAC workflow.
5. Tax shelters, reportable transactions, promoted credit claims, PPLI/PPA, multiple-trust
   stacking, and grantor-trust freezes require an IRS-adversarial review before use.
