## Benchmark Takeaways

Creative Planning's public architecture is a useful model because it organizes complex
planning by client need rather than by professional silo. Its sitemap groups services
into family office, wealth strategies, business solutions, insights, specialty services,
tools/calculators, client services, and legal/privacy pages.

The pages reviewed emphasize integrated planning across investments, tax, estate planning,
risk management, business interests, family governance, philanthropy, accounting, payroll,
audit, legal, technology, valuation, M&A, retirement plans, and business tax.

## Intake Flow

1. Profile the client: founder, business owner, investor/sponsor, UHNW family, W-2 executive,
   real-estate owner, professional practice, nonprofit/institutional, or specialty segment.
2. Capture balance sheet: operating businesses, founder stock, marketable securities, real estate,
   retirement assets, insurance, trusts, debt, private fund interests, crypto/digital assets.
3. Capture goals: tax reduction, asset protection, estate transfer, capital raise, exit,
   succession, liquidity, investor reporting, compliance cleanup, or ongoing family office.
4. Route to lanes: every matter gets a lead lane, secondary lanes, required seats, and gates.
5. Build the integrated plan: entity chart, tax roadmap, investor/capital plan, estate/trust plan,
   asset-protection plan, compliance calendar, and dashboard.
6. Red-team before publishing: IRS, SEC, creditor, fiduciary, valuation, and investor lenses.

## Lane Handoff Rules

- Founder stock or QSBS always triggers Founder / Unicorn Advisory plus Investor Capital Raise
  if money is being raised.
- Trusts plus concentrated stock trigger Estate / Trust / Wealth Transfer and direct-ownership
  carve-out review before funding.
- Business owners with exit or succession issues trigger M&A / Exit / Succession and Tax Planning.
- Credits or incentives in investor materials trigger Credits and Incentives plus Investor
  Capital Raise.
- UHNW families with several entities/trusts trigger Family Office / UHNW plus Accounting/CFO
  reporting.
- International persons, entities, beneficiaries, or assets trigger International and FinCEN.

## Creative-Planning-Inspired Gaps to Close in GLAW

   not a single tax question.
2. Add recurring review cycles for tax-law changes, family events, liquidity events,
   trust-administration events, and investor-reporting dates.
3. Add an integrated family office dashboard that combines investments, entities, trusts,
   accounting, tax deadlines, insurance, and governance.
4. Add a business-owner exit-readiness lane that links valuation, tax, M&A, succession,
   estate planning, and post-exit investment policy.
5. Add investor-purpose packaging for every raise: securities exemption, tax attributes,
   risk factors, suitability, reporting promises, and compliance calendar.
