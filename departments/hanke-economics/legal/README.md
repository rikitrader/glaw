# Legal and institutional source index

Economic reform analysis must identify the legal instrument that created, constrained, or ended a regime. This index is deliberately separate from economic evidence.

For every instrument, record jurisdiction, official title, enactment date, publication source, operative provisions, reserve/convertibility rule, banking and lender-of-last-resort powers, fiscal-financing restrictions, transition provisions, amendment/repeal history, and any court or constitutional challenge.

`UNVERIFIED` means a lead only. It cannot support a legal conclusion. A public PDF or official gazette copy must pass source verification before it is used.

Retrieval failures are recorded in `retrieval-attempts.jsonl`; an HTTP denial or DNS failure never upgrades a legal lead and never creates a citation.
