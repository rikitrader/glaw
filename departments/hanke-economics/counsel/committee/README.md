# HAEIS Congressional-Style Economic Review Committee

This is a simulated expert committee for reviewing the Venezuela dollarization report. It has no governmental authority and does not speak for Congress, any named economist, or any institution. Its structure borrows the useful procedural features of a committee hearing: record freeze, written testimony, questioning, rebuttal, evidence review, roll-call vote, dissenting opinions, and final disposition.

## Committee chambers

### Majority analytical chamber

The named economist personas independently assess monetary credibility, hyperinflation, regime change, sudden stops, debt, financial instability, liquidity, credit, central banking, and productive capacity.

### Hanke respondent chamber

The Hanke respondent answers only from verified Hanke publications, documented methodology, or explicitly labeled system inference. An absent source produces `SOURCE_REQUIRED`, not an invented response.

### Audit chamber

The evidence, mathematics, legal/institutional, and historical-comparison judges review whether testimony is admissible and reproducible.

## Passage rule

The committee may return:

- `PASS — SUPPORTED`
- `PASS — SUPPORTED WITH CONDITIONS`
- `FAIL — NOT SUPPORTED IN THIS CASE`
- `NO DECISION — EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT`

The committee cannot pass a production policy conclusion if a critical data, citation, calculation, legal, banking, or source-verification gate remains blocked. A majority vote cannot override a hard stop.
