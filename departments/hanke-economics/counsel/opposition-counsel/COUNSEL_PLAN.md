# Opposition Economics Counsel — Venezuela Dollarization Review Plan

## Mandate

Review the thesis-grade Venezuela dollarization report as an independent opposing counsel. The counsel must attempt to defeat the report’s Hanke-centered mechanisms, not by caricature, but by testing evidence, accounting, causality, institutional capacity, distributional effects, legal implementation, and counterfactual alternatives.

## Review sequence

1. **Freeze the record.** Assign a report ID, source-vintage cutoff, document manifest, and unresolved-data register.
2. **Identify report claims.** Extract every material claim, formula, scenario assumption, historical analogy, Hanke attribution, and policy condition by section ID.
3. **Steelman Hanke.** Reconstruct the strongest documented Hanke position before opposition begins. Unsupported attribution is blocked.
4. **Convene the counsel.** Run Friedman, Cagan, Sargent, Calvo, Reinhart/Rogoff, Minsky, Bagehot, Bernanke, Goodhart, Hausmann, and the existing HAEIS postures.
5. **Cross-examine the evidence.** For every claim, ask: source? date? unit? locator? transformation? contradictory source? what would falsify it?
6. **Cross-examine the mechanics.** Audit monetary base, M1/M2 conversion, reserve coverage, bank liquidity, capital, fiscal financing, oil FX, balance of payments, debt service, and legal conversion.
7. **Run declared stresses.** Deposit withdrawals 5–50%; oil-price and production grid; capital flight; external financing stop; debt restructuring; sanctions/payment disruption; credit contraction 0–50%.
8. **Run counterfactuals.** Compare immediate dollarization, gradual dollarization, currency board, dual currency, managed float, hard peg, currency basket, and reform without dollarization.
9. **Issue posture opinions.** Each posture must provide a steelman, attack, evidence, mechanism, falsifier, residual risk, and status.
10. **Final arbitration.** Three judges separately assess evidence, mathematics, and economic theory. A counsel victory is not declared merely because it raises a plausible objection.
11. **Publish the opposition review.** The final review must state what the counsel disproved, what it only weakened, what Hanke’s framework survives, and what remains unresolved.

## Final decision rule

- `SUPPORTED`: Hanke framework survives the opposing counsel on verified evidence and reproduced calculations.
- `SUPPORTED_WITH_CONDITIONS`: the framework survives only with specified fiscal, banking, reserve, legal, or transition conditions.
- `NOT_SUPPORTED_IN_THIS_CASE`: the application fails on verified empirical, accounting, legal, or structural grounds.
- `UNRESOLVED`: critical evidence, calculation, or legal records remain unavailable or contradictory.

The current Venezuela evidence bundle should begin as `UNRESOLVED`; counsel cannot convert missing data into an adverse finding against Hanke or into a favorable finding for dollarization.
