# Persona Debate Protocol

## Debate roles

Each economist agent appears as a simulated analytical persona derived from public scholarship. The agent must state its scope and cannot claim private motives, private beliefs, or an actual conversation with the named economist.

## Required turn structure

### 1. Counsel opening

- Identify the report section and claim under review.
- State the strongest version of Hanke’s argument.
- State the posture’s strongest objection.
- Name the evidence needed to decide the issue.

### 2. Cross-examination question

The posture asks one precise question. Questions must be answerable by evidence, accounting, law, or a declared model—not by rhetorical preference.

### 3. Hanke response

The Hanke respondent must classify the answer:

- `HANKE-DIRECT` — verified Hanke text answers the question.
- `HANKE-FRAMEWORK` — the answer follows from a documented methodology.
- `SYSTEM-INFERENCE` — HAEIS extends the framework.
- `SOURCE_REQUIRED` — no verified Hanke response is available.
- `CONCEDE` — the objection remains valid or unresolved.

No “Steve Hanke would say” language is permitted.

### 4. Rebuttal

The posture identifies whether the answer:

- resolves the objection;
- narrows it to a condition;
- shifts the issue to another agent;
- fails to answer it; or
- remains unresolved because data are missing.

### 5. Falsifier

Each posture states the observation, legal fact, calculation, or historical result that would change its view.

### 6. Judge disposition

The evidence judge, mathematics judge, and theory judge independently classify the exchange. A disagreement is preserved.

## Mandatory question families

1. What exactly is the proposition being attributed to Hanke?
2. Which source and locator support it?
3. What is the conversion object: cash, base, M1, M2, or bank liabilities?
4. Who supplies liquidity during a bank run?
5. What fiscal adjustment replaces monetary financing and seigniorage?
6. What happens after a 30% deposit withdrawal and a 40% oil-revenue shock together?
7. What is the external adjustment mechanism after a sudden stop?
8. How are Republic, PDVSA, arbitration, bond, and enforcement claims separated?
9. What law authorizes conversion of contracts, wages, pensions, deposits, loans, and taxes?
10. What productive-capacity constraint remains after nominal stabilization?
11. What evidence would falsify the Hanke framework in Venezuela?
12. What evidence is simply unavailable, and why is no conclusion allowed from that absence?
