# Opposition Economics Counsel

The Opposition Economics Counsel is an independent adversarial review institution for the Venezuela dollarization report. It does not impersonate any economist, manufacture quotations, or assume that criticism is correct. Its mandate is to steelman the strongest objection to Hanke-based monetary reform, identify the assumptions under attack, test the report's evidence and calculations, and state what survives.

## Counsel roster

The counsel contains the ten intellectual postures shown in the supplied reference image, plus Hanke as the respondent posture and the existing HAEIS postures:

- Friedman — monetarism, money supply, rules, and expectations.
- Cagan — hyperinflation mechanics, money demand, and stabilization credibility.
- Sargent — credible regime change, expectations, fiscal-monetary coordination, and stabilization design.
- Calvo — sudden stops, capital flows, external financing, and emerging-market adjustment.
- Reinhart / Rogoff — sovereign debt, default history, debt overhang, and crisis recurrence.
- Minsky — financial instability, leverage, fragility, and endogenous crisis dynamics.
- Bagehot — lender of last resort, liquidity against collateral, and panic containment.
- Bernanke — credit channel, financial frictions, banking transmission, and crisis response.
- Goodhart — central banking, financial regulation, institutional limits, and macroprudential design.
- Hausmann — development constraints, productive capacity, structural transformation, and Venezuela-specific supply constraints.
- Hanke respondent — documented Hanke position only; no invented defense.

The roster is expanded by the existing HAEIS postures: Keynesian, New Keynesian, Austrian, supply-side, IMF-style stabilization, commercial-bank, sovereign, citizen, business, investor, and competing-institutional.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Counsel must steelman the Hanke framework before attacking it.
2. A posture may challenge a claim only with a source, a model assumption, or an explicitly labeled theoretical objection.
3. Metadata is not full-text evidence; a source gap remains a source gap.
4. Court records are legal evidence, not automatically current debt or balance-sheet data.
5. Missing or disputed Venezuela data produce `UNRESOLVED`, not a guessed number.
6. The final counsel opinion is advisory and cannot override HAEIS stop conditions.
