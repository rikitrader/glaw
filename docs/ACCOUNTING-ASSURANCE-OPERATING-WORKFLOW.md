# Enterprise Accounting & Assurance Operating Workflow

This department handles F500-scale accounting work as controlled work product. It
separates technical research from implementation, preserves evidence, and requires
licensed accounting or audit review before an external filing, audit conclusion, or
control certification is released.

## End-to-end lane

```text
ERP / subledgers / contracts / deal data
        ↓
SAP or Oracle extracts + consolidation-system close + source reconciliation
        ↓
US GAAP (ASC) / PCAOB OR IFRS / ISA framework selection
        ↓
Technical research + authoritative evidence + fact pattern reconstruction
        ↓
Revenue / lease / consolidation / M&A transaction accounting analysis
        ↓
Journal entries + reference financial statements + technical accounting memo
        ↓
SOX control design/testing + external/internal audit workpapers
        ↓
Audit-management package + review notes + disclosure and filing controls
        ↓
Controller / CAO / audit committee approval
```

## Workstreams

- Financial reporting: close, consolidations, eliminations, disclosures, reference statements, and tie-outs.
- Technical accounting: ASC Codification, IFRS, revenue recognition, leases, consolidation, and transaction accounting.
- Audit and assurance: PCAOB/ISA-aligned evidence, substantive testing, control testing, findings, remediation, and workpaper indexing.
- Systems and research: ERP/consolidation lineage and dated evidence from approved research tools such as Bloomberg Tax and RIA Checkpoint. Tool access is never represented when it was not provided.
- Controls and SOX: risk-control matrices, key-control design, walkthroughs, testing, deficiency evaluation, remediation, and management certification.
- Transaction accounting: purchase accounting, fair value, goodwill, earn-outs, debt/equity classification, revenue/lease impacts, and close-date entries.

## Judgment gate

The rubric must distinguish a defensible accounting conclusion from textbook recall. A
senior-review quality artifact identifies the controlling literature and effective date,
maps it to the exact facts, addresses contrary guidance and policy elections, quantifies
the financial-statement and control impact, identifies audit evidence, documents
materiality and uncertainty, and states the approval/escalation path. Generic citations,
unsupported system assertions, or an answer that ignores disclosure and controls is
`REVIEW` or `BLOCK`.

## Required evidence package

```text
fact_pattern · source ledger · standards/version/effective date · research excerpts
contract/deal extracts · calculations · journal entries · disclosures
control objectives/testing · audit evidence index · open judgments · reviewer sign-off
```

No AI-generated memo, workpaper, financial statement, or control assessment is a final
audit opinion, management certification, or accounting sign-off.
